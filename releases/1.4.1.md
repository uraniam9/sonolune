# SonoLune 1.4.1

**Quiet stability for Mind and Night** · 6 May 2026

A focused refinement release that fixes Mind Programmer overlay flicker, protects Living Moments audio continuity, and makes Night exit feel cleaner under the thumb.

## Highlights

- Mind Programmer text flashes are smoother and calmer
- Living Moments no longer stop audio when returning to Mind Suite
- Night long-press exit ring is clearer and easier to see

## Mind Suite Fixes

- Mind Programmer text flashes now feel smoother, with no distracting flicker in the Android notification area.
- Living Moments now keep their sound when you leave Mind Suite and come back.
- Space Moments and Living Moments now feel more consistent when returning to Mind Suite.

## Night Exit Polish

- The Night long-press exit ring now appears higher above your thumb, so it is easier to see while holding.
- The ring has a richer lunar amber look with glow, orbit accents, a crescent center, and a soft completion flash.
- Exiting Night Environment now feels more polished without changing the gesture itself.
