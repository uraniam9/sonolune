# SonoLune 2.1.1

**Small repairs, so 2.1.0 keeps the promises it made** · 6 Aug 2026

A quiet follow-up to 2.1.0, made of things that were said and then quietly not done. The Night door kept the hour you chose instead of resetting itself to nine every time Help was opened. An invitation that cannot land now arrives anyway and says what is in the way, rather than disappearing without a word. The Hours can be switched on whenever you like, in Night, mid session, or with the field held, and asks only for the light window it genuinely needs. A moment arriving at its hour no longer takes Night's screen or interrupts a focus session, and a Mind arc now asks for that same window before it promises to lay a moment down. Tapping the Night badge from a full-screen page opens Night instead of an empty screen, and so does entering any suite from one — the empty body under a correct suite bar is gone, along with the blink that changing suites left behind. The back gesture no longer walks out of Night by accident, nor does nothing at all during a focus session. Setting an arc on a time another arc already holds now says so first, and lets you keep both if that is what you meant. And support pages answer the question you arrived with. The one new thing is small: patronage that begins outside the app now says so when it arrives. Nothing is taken away.

## Highlights

- The Night door keeps your hour. Opening Help & Guides was quietly resetting it to nine o'clock, so a door set for another time never arrived when it was meant to.
- An invitation that cannot land now arrives and says why, rather than disappearing without a word. A door only knocks, so it waits for very little — the room it already offers, or a focus session in progress. An Hour settles a moment rather than offering a room, so it answers to more.
- The Hours can be switched on whenever you like — while Night is on, mid focus session, or with the field held. It asks for one thing only, the light window, because a moment without it arrives half-drawn.
- A moment arriving at its hour, or tapped from Arrivals, no longer takes Night's screen or interrupts a focus session. It waits and says what is holding the room, rather than pulling it out from under you.
- A Mind arc — a Living Moment or a Space — now asks for the light window like every other arc that draws. It was scheduling without one and then arriving as sound alone, with nothing to explain the missing colour.
- Tapping "Night is waiting" from the Field Journal, Arrivals or Field backup now opens Night rather than an empty screen.
- Setting an arc for a time another arc already holds says so before saving, for every kind of arc, and lets you keep both if that is what you meant.
- SonoLune notices a new version more reliably, having previously asked the Play Store only once and given up if the answer was slow.
- Light always leaves with the app. Its tint could be left behind after a force stop, resting over every screen, Settings included, until the phone was restarted.
- Support pages now answer the question you arrived with, and a supporter whose included months have settled is no longer told what is missing and given no way to reach it.
- Entering a suite from a full-screen page no longer lands on an empty body beneath a correct suite bar. This is the one that failed testing twice: the routing was right every time, which is exactly why it took so long to find.
- Suites now snap when you change them. Three rounds of trying to animate that change from the frame around the rooms produced, in turn, a blank screen, a black blink, a replay on the way back and a slide in the wrong direction. The motion is gone and the rooms simply change.
- The back gesture no longer walks out of Night by accident, and no longer does nothing at all during a focus session. Both now name what is holding the room, and a second press still closes SonoLune.
- An Hour no longer arrives while the Presence Field is settled. The Night door and The Hours also close themselves if the light window is withdrawn later, rather than sitting on, aimed at hours they cannot keep.
- The download is about a megabyte smaller, and nothing was taken out of the app to make it so.

## Doors that keep their hour

- The Night door kept resetting itself to nine o'clock. Opening Help & Guides re-saved the default over the hour you had chosen, so a door set for eleven was never going to arrive at eleven — and only Night, which is why Focus and a gentle wake seemed fine.
- An invitation that cannot land now arrives and names what is in the way, rather than vanishing without a word. Vanishing was the one thing this feature was rebuilt to stop doing.
- It still stays silent in the one case where speaking is pointless — offering you the room you are already standing in. A Night invitation during Night is noise, and noise is what gets a whole channel muted.
- A door and an Hour answer to different things, and treating them alike had made one of them lie. A door only knocks: it offers you a room, so a suspended space is no obstacle to the offer. Yet every door held back while your space was suspended arrived saying "Your space is held. Long-press the moon in Mind to return" — naming an obstacle that was not in the way, and asking for a step that was not needed. A door now waits for two things only: the room it is offering, and a focus session in progress.
- An Hour settles a moment rather than offering a room, so every authority applies to it — a held space, a settled Presence Field, a withdrawn light window, Night, and a running focus session. All five, and deliberately more than any door asks for.
- The Presence Field is one of those authorities now, and its absence was a hole rather than a decision. The field settles over the screen without suspending anything around it, so nothing was watching for it. An Hour could arrive, be tapped, and then be turned away by the very guard that should have stopped it being offered.
- An invitation that could only arrive as a note does not spend one of the day's few. It never offered its moment, so it does not count as though it had.
- The Hours refused the switch for reasons that would be long over by the time the hour came: being in Night, sitting in a focus session, holding the field. It now tells apart what is permanent from what is passing. Only a missing light window stops the switch, because an Hour truly cannot work without one; everything else is handled at the hour itself, where it belongs. Turning it on no longer depends on what you happened to be doing at the time.
- Switching a door on from inside the room it opens now says what will happen — the door is set, and it opens at your hour next time. It used to take the switch in silence, which read as nothing having happened at all.
- The Night door and The Hours now close themselves if the light window is withdrawn after they were switched on. Both used to sit reading ON, aimed at hours they could never keep. The Hours closes itself at launch too, so it heals without anyone thinking to open Help. It only ever closes: restoring the permission never switches a feature back on behind you.

## Small repairs

- A moment tapped from Arrivals, or arriving on its own hour, could take Night's screen or interrupt a running focus session. It now waits and names what is holding the room, the same way an invitation does.
- A Mind arc — a Living Moment or a Space — was scheduled without ever asking for the light window, then arrived as sound with no colour and nothing to say why. It asks up front now, as Light and Night arcs always have. Sound arcs still do not ask, because they touch no pixels.
- Tapping "Night is waiting" from a full-screen page — the Field Journal, Arrivals, Field backup — opened an empty screen with the suite bar showing Night. It opens Night now, in a single move.
- Setting an arc for a time another arc of the same kind already holds now says so before saving. Three wake arcs at seven in the morning rang three times over each other, and nothing said so until they did. It warns and lets you keep both: two arcs at one minute is a choice, not a mistake.
- SonoLune asks the Play Store about a new version on every launch. It used to ask once per run and give up if the answer was slow, which on a phone that keeps the app in memory could mean never asking again.
- Light's tint could be left behind when SonoLune was force stopped, resting over every app — Settings included — until the phone was restarted. Light now leaves with the app, however it is closed.
- Some moons, seeds and small marks were arriving as stray letters instead of themselves. They are back to being what they were meant to be.

## The room you asked for

- Entering a suite from a full-screen page — the Field Journal, Arrivals, Help, Field backup — could land on a correct suite bar wrapped around an empty body. It failed testing twice and took several wrong answers to find, because the routing was right every single time: the room was asked for correctly and then simply not drawn. Suites are now drawn once, in one place, and the empty body is gone with the cause of it.
- Suites now snap. The frame around the rooms spent three rounds trying to animate a suite arriving and produced, each time, something worse than no animation at all: a blank screen, then a black blink, then a replay on the way back from Help, then a slide in the wrong direction. The motion only ever existed to dress up a change the app already declares to be instant, so it has been taken out and the rooms simply change.
- That is a real loss, and worth naming rather than hiding: changing suites is plainer than it was. Motion belongs to the rooms themselves rather than to the frame around them, and the way to give it back properly is written down for whoever does it next.
- The back gesture no longer walks out of Night. Night has deliberate exits — pull down from the top, hold anywhere — because leaving it should take intent, and an edge swipe is the least deliberate thing on a phone. Back now names what is holding the room instead of moving.
- Back during a focus session used to do nothing whatsoever and say nothing about why. It now names the session, in the same words the rest of this release uses for the same thing.
- Neither is a trap. A second press still closes SonoLune through the door it always had, and the message says so plainly, so nobody is left pressing back at a room that will not let go. Closing the app ends neither the night nor the session — both are held underneath and carry on.

## Support, answering the question you asked

- A supporter whose included months have settled, tapping a session made after them, was told it arrived after their window and then given nothing to do about it. The way to continue is now there.
- The support page leads with whichever answer your arrival actually needs: the patronage when you came from a Lab session, since that is the one thing it opens, and the one-time tiers for everything else, since everything else is bought once and kept.
- Patronage that begins outside SonoLune — finished in the Play Store, or given to you — is now noticed when you come back, and named once. It used to open the Lab in silence, so the one moment worth marking passed without a word.
- The patron arrival card offered to open the Lab and opened the Labs page instead — experiments, Start on boot, the Quick Settings tile. The sessions a patronage actually opens live in Arrivals. Two rooms one letter apart; it now opens the one it names.
- A grandfathered supporter tapping "Become a patron" could get nothing at all, the button having closed the page it then needed in order to move. That was the only route to patronage anyone grandfathered had.
- Nothing changed about who has what. No price moved, nothing became a subscription, and everyone who supported before still owns every session, past and future, forever.

## Underneath

- The download is about a megabyte smaller. Nothing was taken out of the app to manage it — the build simply stopped carrying pieces that nothing referenced.
- The purchase path moved to Google's current billing library. Nothing about it changed on your side: no price moved, nothing you own became something you rent, and every entitlement is where you left it.
