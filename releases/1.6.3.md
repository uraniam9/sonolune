# SonoLune 1.6.3

**Quick Settings, Focus Arc, and outside-app polish** · 2 Jun 2026

A focused continuity release for Quick Settings tile setup, launcher shortcuts, permission returns, Focus completion language, Focus Arc scheduling, Labs layout, support gates, and small Sound Atmosphere wording polish.

## Highlights

- Labs can now request the SonoLune Light Quick Settings tile directly on Android 13 and newer
- Quick Settings tile setup remembers when Android reports the tile was added or already present
- Launcher shortcuts now use clearer SonoLune action language and no longer expose Labs
- Direct launcher shortcuts can open their target before first-run onboarding
- Permission screens now return more naturally to the same SonoLune page after Android Back
- Focus completion copy is calmer, with less repeated held-language
- Focus completion now offers a Schedule Focus Arc action that opens Lunar Arc directly to a Focus Arc draft
- Free Night Arc timing guidance keeps its Support action while hiding sooner
- Support gently extends now names Lunar Arc scheduled suite actions
- Labs now uses a more consistent card language for Quick Settings, Mind Programmer, and system surface light
- Soundsoft Lab Sessions copy is softened while preserving the release-status message

## Quick Settings and Labs

- Added a Quick Settings tile setup card inside Labs for SonoLune Light.
- On Android 13 and newer, SonoLune can ask Android to add the Light tile directly.
- If Android reports the tile was already present, Labs now shows an already-added state.
- The remembered Quick Settings state survives navigation and cold starts after Android confirms added or already added.
- Labs now presents Quick Settings, Mind Programmer, and system surface light as consistent experimental cards instead of a mixed card/list surface.

## Launcher Shortcuts

- Removed Labs from launcher shortcuts to avoid edge-case entry paths.
- Renamed shortcuts to clearer action wording such as Open Focus Session, Adjust Light Suite, Open Lune Field, and Open Mind Suite.
- Lune Field shortcut now opens the Lune Field surface instead of the general Night page.
- Direct launcher shortcut opens can bypass first-run onboarding for that intentional shortcut route.

## Permissions

- System-window, accessibility, exact alarm, write settings, and DND permission screens now stay in SonoLune’s Android task flow more naturally.
- After granting or reviewing Android permissions, Back should return closer to the same SonoLune page instead of feeling like a lost scroll or cold return.

## Focus Completion

- Reduced repeated held-language on the Focus completion screen.
- Focus completion now says Focused for the session length instead of You held focus.
- Eye Rest completion now names Lune Guard as the quiet eye reset.
- The daily summary now says focused moments completed today instead of repeating held phrasing.
- Removed the generic your focus space will be ready again tomorrow line.
- Added Schedule Focus Arc from the completion summary so the next session can be prepared in advance.
- The Schedule Focus Arc action routes to Lunar Arc and opens a Focus Arc draft directly.

## Lunar Arc and Support

- Free Night Arc custom-timing guidance now auto-hides sooner so it does not sit over bottom controls for too long.
- The Free Night Arc guidance keeps its Support action so users can still open the Lunar Arc support gate from the message.
- The Support gently extends section now includes Lunar Arc scheduled suite actions for Focus, Sound, Light, Mind, Night, and Lune Guard.

## Sound Atmosphere

- Soundsoft Lab Sessions card language now avoids technical cache/open wording and reads more like SonoLune.
- The empty Soundsoft Lab status message remains the familiar release-ready line for incoming Atmosphere Sessions.
