# SonoLune 1.9.6

**The Presence Field made dependable, arcs that rest together, and a calmer edge on every screen** · 30 Jun 2026

*An Insider build: it went to testers and never reached the public store.*

A quiet insider build with no new rooms, just the whole field made steadier and softer to live in. The Presence Field is the heart of it, a field you armed and left now genuinely returns after a cold start, re-drawing over your other apps the moment the screen and its draw-over permission settle, rather than the room reading "active" while nothing reached the apps; and a per-app field now re-engages the instant you come back to a chosen app from Recents, no longer waiting for a screen-off or a fresh tap to notice you have returned. When a per-app field is set to release, a single soft word now floats over the app about a minute before it lets go, so the quieting is a gentle heads-up rather than a surprise, never a ticking deadline. Scheduled Lunar Arcs now keep their own place in the notification shade and gather into one calm group, instead of each new one clearing the last. And running underneath is a broad pass of calm edges: the navigation beam beneath the active suite melts into the surface at both ends rather than ending in a hard bar, Light's living-colour rails fade softly at their edges instead of clipping a card mid-word, Settle and "Match light & sound" rest quietly until they are the thing in effect, and a Focus room previews its colour across the whole screen the moment you tap it. A quiet way to reopen each suite's walkthrough now rides in Light, Sound, Focus, Lunar Arc and Night. The Presence Field, SonoLune's flagship room, now carries a deeper guide to the calm science beneath it, framed plainly as a visual detox that drains colour to loosen the pull and lets the loud apps go boring, and its depth slider gives a soft, felt detent as it crosses each named quiet; its light control in the notification steps past the field in a single tap when it is holding; and the Kindle-paper grain keeps its place across a cold start rather than needing a manual toggle. Nothing here changes what anything does, it is the same SonoLune, simply more dependable and more whole at the edges.

## Highlights

- The Presence Field returns on a cold start: a field you armed and left now actually re-draws over your other apps when you reopen SonoLune, instead of only reading "active" in the room while the apps stayed untouched until you lifted and re-entered it.
- A per-app field re-engages from Recents: returning to a chosen app through the recents switcher now re-applies the field at once, no longer waiting for a screen-off or reopening the app, through Usage Access alone, with no accessibility needed.
- A gentle word before the field lets go: when a per-app field is set to release, a soft cue floats over the app about a minute before, so the screen quieting is never a surprise, a calm heads-up, not a per-second countdown.
- Arcs that rest together: scheduled Lunar Arcs now keep their own place in the notification shade and bundle into one quiet group, instead of each new one clearing the one before.
- A calmer navigation beam: the soft line beneath the active suite now melts into the surface at both ends rather than ending in a hard bar, the same blended beam for every suite.
- Light's colour rails fade at their edges: Field colour, Colour chorus and the build-your-own cycle now melt out softly at the strip edges instead of clipping a card mid-word.
- Buttons that rest until you use them: Settle in Light and "Match light & sound" in Focus sit quietly until they are the light or room in effect, then warm to show they are active.
- Tap a Focus room to preview it: choosing a room now washes its colour across the whole screen at once, so you can feel the mood before you match your light and sound.
- Your saved soundscape, remembered: a saved Sound atmosphere comes back highlighted after a cold start, so you can see at a glance which one is resting in place.
- A guide always within reach: Light, Sound, Focus, Lunar Arc and Night each gained a quiet way to reopen their walkthrough at any time, not only on the first visit.
- The flagship room, deepened: the Presence Field's guide now tells the calm science beneath it, a visual detox that drains colour to loosen the pull, lets the loud apps go quietly boring, and nudges rather than walls, and the field is marked plainly as SonoLune's flagship.
- A felt hand on the depth: the Presence depth slider now gives a soft, gentle detent as it crosses into each named quiet, Softening, Settled, Still, Deep quiet, so you can feel the field deepen without watching the screen.
- One tap past the field: the SonoLune notification's light control now steps past a holding Presence Field in a single tap, and its icon quietly shows whether Light, the Field, or a pause is what is in effect.
- Paper grain that keeps its place: the Kindle-paper finish now survives a cold start when a warm scene is restored with it, re-drawing itself as things settle instead of needing a manual toggle, and it rests with a calm word while accessibility surfacing is on, since one overlay carries the grain or the surfacing, not both.
- Presence Field begins to earn its keep: the core field and one chosen app stay free, while reaching the living field across every app you choose, and setting a free-use window before the quiet, become Supporter refinements. Instant timing stays free.
- Night tuning gathers under Supporter: intensity, arrival, and the offline sun and location timing now open with Supporter Night, alongside Moon Haze, Midnight Drift and Moon Shimmer, while turning the core Night field on and off stays free for everyone.
- A calmer first run: the guides no longer open on their own each time you visit a suite. Every suite carries an always-there way in (the question-mark, or the Help hub), so the walkthrough opens only when you ask.
- The support sheet, brought up to date: it now names everything support opens, including the deeper Presence Field, deeper Light, saved soundscapes, Night tuning, and the fuller Lune Guard and Lune Field.
- Small clarities: the shared field poster keeps its badges inside the frame at every font size, the Open Window preview explains itself on a tap, and a run of introduced em dashes was eased back out of the copy.
- A moment kept only when you mean it: the gentle offer to remember how you have tuned a Space or Living Moment now waits for a change you actually made, measured against that moment's own settled recipe, so simply returning to a moment you left untouched no longer raises the ask, and each different moment can still be offered in its own turn rather than one asking once and quieting the rest for the sitting.
- A felt nudge to look away: turn on Deepening pulse and the per-app field firms up a gentle haptic in your hand as it closes toward hardly-visible, a cue that reaches you inside the app itself. And the grace period, how long a quick glance away is forgiven before the quiet resets, is now yours to set: Brief, Standard, or Lenient.
- A tidier Night: Moon Shimmer, a purely decorative sky glow, moved out of Advanced Atmosphere to sit with the everyday toggles, and the advanced section now holds the two that truly shape the field, Midnight Deep Drift and Moon Haze.
- Mind wears the signature: the centre tab, your home, now carries SonoLune's own moon rather than a generic figure, so the still point of the bar is the mark itself.
- A guide mark that is yours, and per suite: the floating help mark is now SonoLune's soft sparkle, and reading one suite's guide retires only that suite's mark, so every other space still offers its own until you have read it there too.
- The ongoing notification reads true to the field: it shows the Presence Field's real depth instead of a flat 0 percent, names your sound beside the field whether it is playing or resting, and its light toggle shows the bulb when the environment is open, keeping the field mark for when the field itself is holding the whole screen.

## The Presence Field, made dependable

- A field you armed and left genuinely returns after a cold start, the overlay re-draws over your other apps the moment the screen and the draw-over permission settle, instead of the room showing "active" while nothing actually reached the apps until a manual lift and re-entry.
- A per-app field re-engages when you come back to a chosen app from Recents, not only after a screen-off or reopening it, detected through Usage Access alone, with no accessibility service.
- When a per-app field is set to release, a single soft cue floats over the app about a minute before it lets go, so the wind-down is a gentle word rather than an abrupt drop, and deliberately not a stress-inducing ticking timer.
- The release card now settles cleanly on top of the field the instant it appears, rather than briefly resting behind it; and the Presence room names plainly when the field will release, since a live countdown could never show while you are inside the very app it is timing.
- The SonoLune notification's light control now steps past a holding field in a single tap, a quick way out from the shade, and its icon reflects what is in effect, quietly showing Light, the Field, or a pause.
- As SonoLune's flagship room, the field's guide now tells the calm science beneath the quiet, a visual detox that drains colour to loosen the pull, lets the loud apps go quietly boring, and nudges rather than walls, so you set the phone down on your own, and the field is marked plainly as the flagship. Its depth slider gives a soft, felt detent as it crosses into each named quiet, so you can feel it deepen without watching.
- The ongoing notification tells the truth about the field: it shows the field's real depth rather than a flat 0 percent, names your sound beside it whether it plays or rests, and its light toggle shows the bulb when the environment is open, keeping the field mark for when a whole-screen field is genuinely holding.

## Arcs that rest together, and a clearer journal

- Lunar Arcs that fire now keep a steady place in the notification shade and gather into one quiet group, so several can rest there together instead of each new one clearing the one before.
- The Field Journal now says plainly that it gathers both public releases and the insider builds between them, from v1.0 onward, so the 1.9 series is no longer described as "public".

## A calmer edge, across every suite

- The navigation beam beneath the active suite fades to nothing at both ends, melting into the surface rather than ending in a hard line, one shared beam, so every suite reads the same.
- Light's living-colour rails, Field colour, Colour chorus and the build-your-own cycle, fade softly at their edges, so a card melts out instead of clipping mid-word.
- Settle in Light and "Match light & sound" in Focus rest quietly until they are the light or room currently in effect, then warm to show they are active, so they read as something to reach for, not a permanent glow.
- Choosing a Focus room previews its colour across the whole running screen at once, before you decide to match your light and sound to it.
- The Kindle-paper grain keeps its place across a cold start when a warm scene is restored with it, re-drawing itself as support and the overlay settle, rather than waiting for a manual off-and-on, and it rests with a calm word while accessibility surfacing is on, since a single overlay can carry the grain or the surfacing, not both.
- The centre of the suite bar, Mind, now carries SonoLune's own moon rather than a generic figure, so the still home of the bar is the mark itself, and the floating help affordance became SonoLune's soft sparkle, one that retires per suite as you read each guide rather than all at once.

## A moment kept only when you mean it

- When you adjust the light or sound of a Space or Living Moment, SonoLune may quietly offer to remember it. That offer now rests on a true change you have made, measured against the moment's own settled recipe, so revisiting a moment you left exactly as it was no longer raises the ask out of nowhere.
- And the offer greets each moment in its own turn: keeping the tuning of one moment no longer keeps SonoLune from offering to remember a different one later in the same sitting.

## Support that stays gentle

- Presence Field keeps its whole heart free: arming and lifting the field, the depth, and one chosen app in the living field. Reaching that living field across every app you choose, and setting a window of free use before the quiet, now open with support. Instant timing stays free, so the field still quiets the app that pulls you most.
- Night keeps its core free (turning the field on and off, at a calm default), while the tuning gathers under Supporter Night: intensity, how gently Night arrives, and the offline sun and location timing, alongside Moon Haze, Midnight Drift and Moon Shimmer. Tapping a gated control opens the same calm supporter note as before, never a dead end.
- The support sheet was brought up to date so it names what support actually opens now: the deeper Presence Field, deeper Light (temperature, Focus light presets and the Ambient colour system), saved soundscapes, Night tuning, and the fuller Lune Guard and Lune Field.
- The Open Window, the short free preview of a Supporter refinement, now says what it is on a tap, so a feature you just felt then rested reads as a preview ending, not as something taken away.
