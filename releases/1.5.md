# SonoLune 1.5

**Lunar Arc, Lune Guard, and Lune Field** · 18 May 2026

A calmer way to let SonoLune meet you through the day: scheduled Arc handoffs, eye-care breaks, personal soundscapes, clearer Mind moments, and a more reliable night field.

## Highlights

- Lunar Arc can prepare Focus, Sound, Light, Mind, Night, Reminder, Wake, and Lune Guard moments
- Lune Guard adds guided eye breaks and optional outside-SonoLune reminders
- Supporters can save personal soundscapes and bring them back later
- Mind Arc can queue Space, Living, and Suggested Focus moments in order
- Night, permissions, support, and navigation feel steadier after restarts

## Lunar Arc

- Lunar Arc now prepares different parts of SonoLune at the time you choose.
- You can schedule Wake, Focus, Sound, Light, Mind, Night, Reminder, and Lune Guard actions.
- Today Arcs show what is coming next, with clearer remaining time.
- Permission prompts now explain why each Android permission is needed.
- Tapping an Arc notification opens the right suite with the planned setup already waiting.
- Supporter-only Arc refinements are clearer, while the essential flows stay available.

## Wake and Night

- Wake and Night wording now feels more like SonoLune and less like a generic alarm.
- Wake previews, notification summaries, and alarm permission helper text are clearer.
- Night scheduling and end times are more reliable after the app restarts.
- Night Environment is less likely to remain stuck or leave light effects behind.
- Night Arc now feels like entering an environment, not starting a ringtone.
- Lune Field preview copy and Night text have been cleaned up.

## Sound Atmosphere

- Added supporter saved soundscapes with custom names and suggested preset names.
- Saved soundscapes now persist across cold starts and appear in Sound Atmosphere, Wake Arc, Sound Arc, and Focus ambient field.
- The custom soundscape card stays visible after saving and updates live after save or delete.
- Duplicate preset names and duplicate saved parameter mixes are prevented.
- Improved Sound Arc length syncing, notification stop behavior, and cross-suite playback continuity.

## Light Suite

- Added scheduled Light Arc support with selectable light tuning.
- Added custom light preset support so scheduled light profiles can be more personal.
- Improved light restore behavior, end notifications, permission handling, and day/night contrast in Lunar Arc.
- Refined the philosophy around scheduled light ending: SonoLune can notify and restore intentionally instead of silently fighting user changes.

## Mind Suite

- Added Mind Arc moment selection directly from the Arc editor.
- Mind moments can now be queued and advanced in sequence by their scheduled timing.
- Fixed Mind Arc fallback behavior so user-selected moments are honored instead of hardcoded starts.
- Added Lunar Arc and Lune Guard access inside Mind Suite, positioned beneath Ambient Field.
- Improved scheduled Mind snackbar copy and route context so users know why they arrived.

## Lune Guard

- Added Lune Guard as a standalone eye-care space with guided resets, blink practice, far-field breaks, posture cues, haptics, and daily progress.
- Added Outside SonoLune mode for autonomous eye-break cues across Android when system-window permission is granted.
- Added a one-minute preview for checking outside eye-break cues.
- Improved cue design so outside guidance feels intentional instead of subliminal.
- Simplified completion into a calmer reset state without amateur tick or crowded ring visuals.
- Added Lune Guard support inside Reminder Arc for scheduled care breaks.

## Labs and Permissions

- Audited and refined Labs toggles including quiet invitations, simpler sound blends, light tuning, and learned light patterns.
- Quiet Invitations now use alarm permission for scheduled firing where appropriate.
- Updated system presence permission language with optional alarm and accessibility permissions.
- System presence copy now makes optional permissions clearer and less forceful.

## Support and Purchase Flow

- Fixed support thank-you return flow so users are not trapped after restart or restore.
- Updated supporter gate copy for Lunar Arc, Lune Guard, saved soundscapes, and deeper suite refinements.
- Removed the Most chosen flag from the Supporter tier and encouraged Sustainer and Caretaker without changing feature access.
- Cleaned confusing support copy and made Play Store pricing behavior clearer.

## Navigation and Stability

- SonoLune now remembers the current screen more reliably on cold start.
- Added launcher shortcuts for key spaces.
- Improved Focus and Night interaction, including long-press Focus release while Night Environment is active.
- Improved keyboard handling in Arc note fields.
- Fixed blank screens, stale widgets, duplicate notifications, and several restart/background scheduling problems.
