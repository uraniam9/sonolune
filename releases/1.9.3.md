# SonoLune 1.9.3

**The Light field, refined, a Kindle-paper finish, a screen that can follow your sun, and a gentler way for Night to arrive** · 23 Jun 2026

*An Insider build: it went to testers and never reached the public store.*

This release deepens the Light and Night fields with a handful of calm, long-asked refinements. Paper Matte gains a true paper finish, a tactile e-paper grain laid over the matte surface, like reading on a Kindle, with the Paper Grain slider shaping how present it feels. Night learns to arrive gently: choose how slowly it eases in when it begins, from an instant on to a long, unhurried drift. And if you like, the screen can follow your own sun, set your rough latitude once (worked out offline, with no GPS and nothing sent anywhere) and Auto Night follows your local sunset and sunrise, folding into the body-clock curve it already keeps; or leave it off for a simple evening window. The Light filter now reaches the edges of the screen on its own, the status bar, the navigation bar, and the notch, using only the draw-over-apps permission, so the calm feels whole without asking for accessibility. A new corner of Labs holds three quiet, device-level options: bring the field back after a restart, let the screen dim further toward near-black for pitch-dark rooms, and draw the overlay in software if a display ever shows banding. Telling us something is easier, too: reporting a bug, flagging a disturbance, or suggesting a direction all open one calm in-app note that arrives with the details that help. And the Presence Field and Night settings read more clearly, each gathered into plainly named groups, so you can see at a glance what shapes the field and what reaches across your phone. A few quiet kindnesses round it out: your sound now keeps playing when you swipe SonoLune away, the Light controls gently wait while the Presence Field is settled rather than fighting it for the screen, and the field explains itself more softly throughout.

## Highlights

- A paper finish: Paper Matte gains a tactile e-paper grain, a Kindle-like texture laid over the matte surface. It sits just under the Paper / Matte switch, and the Paper Grain slider shapes how present it feels.
- Night arrives gently: choose how slowly Night eases in when it begins, Off for an instant on, or Gentle, Slow, or a long Drift, so dusk settles in rather than snapping.
- The screen can follow your sun: set your rough latitude (worked out offline, no GPS, nothing sent) and Auto Night follows your local sunset and sunrise; leave it Off for a fixed evening window.
- To the edges, on its own: the Light filter now covers the status bar, navigation bar, and notch using only draw-over-apps, no accessibility needed for the calm to feel whole.
- Start on boot (Labs): if the field was on when your phone powered down, it returns on its own after a restart.
- Deeper dim (Labs): let Screen Brightness dim further toward near-black for pitch-dark rooms, it eases back as you raise brightness.
- Software rendering (Labs): draw the overlay in software instead of on the GPU, a gentle fix if a display ever shows banding or flicker under the filter.
- Easier to tell us something: Report a bug, Report a disturbance, and Shape the future now open one calm in-app note that carries your app and device details, write a line, and it hands off to your mail.
- Clearer to live with: the Presence Field now separates what shapes the field (no permission) from what reaches across your phone (a system permission), under a plain line that the field is system-wide, and when that wider reach is on, its heading quietly lights up to show it is active. Night settings gather into three calm groups, its depth, when it arrives, and how it holds through the night.
- Warmth that reads like firelight: Warm Light now follows a real colour-temperature curve, a gentle warm-white at a touch, deep candlelight at full, and the slider quietly shows the temperature (≈2400K · warm amber) as you move it. Deeper warmth naturally carries less blue.
- Quiet Veil, made its own: it is now a colour-neutral haze that softens glare and harsh contrast without adding any tint, clearly distinct from Paper Matte's warm, textured surface, and it layers cleanly with your warmth instead of doubling it.
- Easier to read at depth: stacking Warm, Quiet Veil and Paper Matte heavily no longer washes text out, the field gently holds the light layers back so words stay legible.
- Night leads everywhere again: entering Night while the Presence Field was limited to a few apps used to leave Night showing only there. Night is a whole-screen environment once more, even with a per-app field armed.
- Quieter returns: Paper finish now carries over your other apps too and takes hold the moment you turn it on, the field eases back gently after you switch away and come back rather than dropping, and the wind-down invitation breathes in softly instead of blinking, now in words that suit the moment, whether the field settled at once or eased in as you stayed. The Night arrival and Sun & location controls each gained a clear Off, and Sun & location now names example regions (UK, US, India and more) when you long-press them.
- Sound that stays with you: your atmosphere keeps playing when you swipe SonoLune away from Recents, and when you open it again, the play button shows it is still going, instead of looking paused.
- Light waits for the field: while the Presence Field is settled over your screen, the Light controls gently step aside, a tap brings a soft "lift the field to tune" note rather than the two quietly fighting over the screen. Lift the field and your tuning returns exactly as you left it.
- The Presence Field, gentler to live with: when SonoLune is resting, tapping "Enter the field" points you to wake it from the moon in Mind; controls paused by Fade to quiet now say so when tapped instead of feeling dead; and Field depth and "Let the room settle" stay available unless Fade to quiet is genuinely steering your chosen apps.
- Steadier at larger text: SonoLune now follows your system Font Size and keeps its screens laid out cleanly as text grows, the dense panels (Light sliders, the Presence Field, Night settings) stay readable and whole instead of overflowing at the largest sizes.

## A paper finish

- Turn on Paper / Matte, then Paper finish just beneath it, for a tactile e-paper grain over the matte, like reading on a Kindle rather than a screen.
- The Paper Grain slider shapes how present both the texture and the diffuse matte feel.
- It shows in the Light suite preview and carries over your other apps when the field is system-wide.

## Night, on your timing and your sun

- Night arrival: pick how gently Night eases in, Off (instant), Gentle, Slow, or Drift, so it settles rather than snaps.
- Sun & location: choose your rough latitude and Auto Night follows your local sunset and sunrise, computed offline with no GPS; pick Off to use a fixed evening window instead.
- Both fold into the body-clock curve Night already keeps, so the depth still deepens through the small hours and eases before morning.

## To the edges of the screen

- The Light filter now covers the status bar, navigation bar, and display cutout on its own, using only the draw-over-apps permission.
- The optional accessibility coverage stays as a fallback, for the pulled-down notification shade and lock screen, and for displays that clip overlays at the very edges.

## Quiet options in Labs

- Start on boot brings the field back after a restart, if it was on when the phone powered down.
- Deeper dim lets Screen Brightness reach further toward near-black for very dark rooms.
- Software rendering draws the overlay in software, try it if the filter shows banding or flicker on your display.

## Easier to reach us, clearer to read

- Report a bug, Report a disturbance, and Shape the future open one calm note in the app; write a line and it opens your mail prefilled with the details that help.
- The Presence Field separates what shapes the field from what reaches across your phone, under a plain line that everything there is system-wide; when that wider reach is on, its heading shows a soft live mark.
- Night settings gather into three labelled groups, Atmosphere, when Night arrives, and through the night, so the panel reads as one calm whole.
- SonoLune follows your system Font Size while keeping its panels from overflowing, so larger text stays readable and cleanly laid out across every screen.

## The field, and your sound, hold steady

- Your sound atmosphere keeps playing when you swipe SonoLune from Recents, and the play button reflects it the next time you open the app.
- The Light controls step aside while the Presence Field is settled, a gentle "lift the field to tune" note instead of a tug-of-war; lifting the field returns your light exactly as it was.
- When SonoLune is resting, "Enter the field" points you to wake it from the moon in Mind, and any control paused by Fade to quiet explains itself on a tap rather than feeling dead.
