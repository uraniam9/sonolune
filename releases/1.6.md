# SonoLune 1.6

**Soundsoft Lab, Night depth, and safer accessibility** · 25 May 2026

A broad refinement release that prepares remote Soundsoft Lab Sessions, improves Night depth control, makes Mind moments more personal, and fixes sensitive accessibility greyscale overlay behavior.

## Highlights

- Sound Atmosphere can now prepare remote Soundsoft Lab Sessions without growing the APK
- Seven new Sound Atmosphere soundscapes expand the ambient field
- Focus Session gained curated Calm Light presets while keeping Calm Glow free
- Focus greyscale and accessibility coverage were reworked to avoid white-wash overlay stacking
- Night Environment gained intensity control, presets, auto deepen, and clearer scheduled-night behavior
- Mind Arc now shows a scrollable queue of Space, Living, and Suggested Focus moments
- Mind moments support Living Veil Glow, time-aware ordering, and Moment Memory updates
- Lune Guard and Quiet Invitations explain outside cues and notification echoes more clearly

## Sound Atmosphere

- Added the foundation for remote Soundsoft Lab Sessions, downloaded only when opened and cached for offline return.
- Added seven curated soundscapes: Forest Rain, Library Rain, Tide Lantern, Warm Wind, Low Storm, Quiet Room, and Lucid Focus.
- Remote session audio is treated as separate ambience layers and can play alone or mix beside Elements and Micro Textures.
- Prepared Sound Atmosphere for verified downloadable ambience that can arrive later without making the app heavier.
- Renamed Sound Packs to Soundsoft Lab Sessions and rewrote empty states in SonoLune language.
- Replaced naked preset symbols on Low Storm, Quiet Room, and related fields with proper icons.
- Focus Ambient Field now sees the newer Sound Atmosphere soundscapes.
- Reworded Atmosphere Depth as a future depth memory instead of a developer placeholder.

## Light and Accessibility

- Fixed Focus greyscale not applying when accessibility coverage is active.
- Fixed a native sync path that dropped the accessibility greyscale flag before it reached the overlay service.
- Prevented accessibility and app overlays from stacking over app content during Focus greyscale.
- Stopped the accessibility overlay from falling back to full-screen when Android does not report system-bar bounds.
- Expanded accessibility coverage for status and navigation bars while keeping the overlay bar-only.
- Improved Light Suite guidance for Color Veil tint depth and already-active accessibility coverage.
- Added curated Focus Calm Light presets with distinct Warm Light, Blue Light Reduction, screen dim, Quiet Veil, Color Veil, Paper Matte, and Living Field recipes.
- Kept default Calm Glow free and unchanged while supporter presets get their own Focus light gate copy.
- Focus Calm Light preset selection now follows the same overlay permission helper flow as the main Calm Light toggle.

## Night Environment

- Restored the intended scheduled-night behavior: scheduled Night offers entry inside SonoLune instead of forcing the environment open.
- Fixed Night badge entry from locked suites so tapping the badge routes into Night Environment instead of leaving a blank locked screen.
- Added Night Intensity so Night depth can be shaped manually.
- Added readable, balanced, and deep Night depth presets.
- Added Auto deepen over the night for a gradual evening-to-late-night softening path.
- Clarified that 0% means the quietest active Night depth, not turning Night off.
- When Auto deepen is active, the manual slider is held without repeated snackbar noise.
- Night timing managed by Lunar Arc now explains where to adjust the Night Arc.

## Mind Moments

- Added Living Veil Glow, a persisted Color Veil intensity control shown only while a Living Moment is active.
- Added time-aware ordering for Space and Living Moments so dusk, night, morning, and day scenes surface more naturally.
- Fixed Space Moment rail jumps caused by using the old fixed order for scroll positioning.
- Expanded Suggested Focus Moments with more practical session options.
- Mind Arc now shows a scrollable queue of all Space Moments, all Living Moments, and more Suggested Focus moments.
- Fixed Mind Arc showing only a small hardcoded set of moments.
- Fixed Moment Memory timing so the update dialog can appear after meaningful light or sound tuning.
- Moment Memory now saves approved tuning back to the active moment and reuses it on future opens.

## Lune Guard and Labs

- Outside SonoLune now explains that the first Lune Guard cue arrives after the selected interval and repeats inside the chosen hours.
- Clarified Breath Cue as the visible system-window field and Quiet Return as the notification echo.
- Quiet Invitations now explain that SonoLune plants one eligible notification echo at a time after use, window, and cooldown checks.
- Renamed Forget learned light patterns to Forget remembered moment tuning.
- Clarified that Moment Memory stores user-approved light and sound tuning for Space and Living Moments.

## Language and Polish

- Renamed Lune Family to Lune Circle.
- Removed internal placeholder and freeze wording from user-facing production copy.
- Kept the original Hold gently to quiet light moment hint while removing overflowing tune text.
- The Shape SonoLune with us page now uses the shared SonoLune brand moon.
- Improved UX across the SonoLune fields, including details that are felt more than listed.
- Improved terminology around Soundsoft Lab, Night depth, Moment Memory, and outside Lune Guard cues.
