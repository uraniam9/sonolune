# SonoLune 1.9.1

**Presence Field settles in, easing out of Focus, breath pauses, a grey wash, and warmth with Night**

*An Insider build: it went to testers and never reached the public store.*

Presence Field grows four gentle ways to meet the rest of your evening, each one optional, off until you choose it, and never a wall. Let the field ease in when a Focus session ends, so the screen settles instead of snapping back to bright. Let a soft breath cue drift by while you rest, tap-through and fading on its own. Wash the screen toward grey when colour feels loud. And when Night is on, let the field lend it a little more amber, Night still leads, and the field only ever adds warmth, never less. Everything composes over the one field you already know, asks for no new permission, and a single tap still lifts it all.

## Highlights

- Ease out of Focus: when a session ends, the field can settle in gently, a soft landing rather than a hard snap back to a bright screen. One tap lifts it.
- Breath pauses: while you rest in the field, a soft Lune Guard breath cue can drift by now and then, never blocking, always tap-through, and it fades on its own.
- Wash to grey: drain colour toward grey while the field holds, for when colour feels loud. It quiets colour rather than removing it fully, and needs no new permission.
- Warm with Night: when Night is on, the field can lend it a little more amber. Night still leads, this only adds warmth, and never lessens Night's protection.
- Every new touch is optional and off until you choose it, so your existing Light, Night, Focus, and arcs stay exactly as they were unless you ask.

## Around the field

- A soft comedown when Focus ends, so rest meets you instead of a bright screen.
- Optional breath pauses that float a gentle cue by and fade on their own, tap-through, never a gate.
- An optional grey wash for when colour feels loud, honest about quieting colour rather than truly removing it.

## Composes with Night

- With Night on, the field can warm it further toward amber while Night keeps leading.
- The warmth is only ever added, it never reduces Night's warm or blue protection.
- As always, the field yields to Focus, and a single tap lifts everything.
