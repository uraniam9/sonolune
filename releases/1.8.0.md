# SonoLune 1.8.0

**Field expansion, Lune Guard intelligence, arc stability, and environmental polish**

The Field Expansion joins the 1.8.0 line: supporter refinements now open as a real thirty-second window before support is ever mentioned, onboarding ends inside a living atmosphere, the Soundsoft Lab Sessions branch stands ready for atmospheres that will arrive over the air with an open layer in every session, quiet invitations graduate out of Labs, and the Connect card learns to tell one moon cycle of presence under tonight's actual moon. Beneath it, the original stability pass remains: Lune Guard cues fire only when the screen is actually on, scheduled arcs activate only when you tap their notification, arc notifications survive ritual reschedules, and Mind Suite performance improved on lower-end devices. A final polish pass adds smooth crossfade looping for every sound clip, corrects night environment notification wiring so the right label always shows immediately, and raises Mind Suite text contrast so the warm dim of night never washes out words on screen.

## Open Window

- Tapping Binaural Beats, Tone Generator, Stereo Width, Atmosphere Depth, or a neuro soundscape as a free listener now opens it for thirty seconds inside your own field instead of showing a wall.
- A countdown capsule in the Neuro Layers and Atmosphere Depth headers now ticks in real time, second by second, while the window is open.
- Ambient System opens the same way: pick a supporter motion mode and the living colour travels the room for thirty seconds, then rests back to its free shape when the window settles.
- When a sound window closes, only the borrowed layers settle out over a slow eight-second fade, your own field keeps playing untouched. Only then is support offered, in context.
- One window per refinement per day, reopening fresh the next day. If you already released what you borrowed, the window closes silently with no gate at all.
- Focus reclaims the field cleanly: starting a session closes any open window without ceremony. Becoming a supporter mid-window simply keeps the field open as yours.

## Deeper Field, Cleaner Edges

- Atmosphere Depth now reaches further: alongside its warmth and room-space, depth adds felt low-end body and a wider, more enveloping field, so raising it is something you feel, not just see.
- Entering or leaving Night refreshes the notification summary immediately with no blank flash and no stale label, the right text appears in the same moment the environment changes.
- Temperature and Colour Veil now rest exactly at zero when you set them there, instead of drifting a hair above as the last light settles.
- Paper Matte returns to a single, honest tap: toggling it opens the support invitation directly, rather than a brief window that could be fumbled against its own slider.
- Granting SonoLune's optional access is guided end to end: on Android 11+ it deep-links straight to SonoLune's own switch, for accessibility coverage, Do Not Disturb, and overlay display, instead of dropping you into a long settings list.
- A gentle coach then shows exactly what to turn on: a card floated over the Settings screen where SonoLune is allowed to draw, or a Toast where it is not, including the Accessibility screen, which hides drawn cards for safety, so the guidance is never lost.
- On Xiaomi, Vivo, and Oppo phones that hold back background pop-ups, SonoLune uses the Toast coach and can open the OEM's background pop-up setting directly; once you allow it, the nicer card returns.
- Returning from a focus reflection eases Mind open with your reflections in view, and never raises a stray completion card over them.
- A held field, set with a long press on the Mind moon, is now honored everywhere: tapping a Light or Sound arc, even from the lock screen or a cold start, offers to return you to your environment instead of quietly applying over your stillness.
- The light preview now shows your Colour Veil tint beside warmth, veil, dim, and matte, so every active tuning reads at a glance.
- The outside Lune Guard breath cue drops its redundant second button, a single Skip closes it cleanly.

## First Atmosphere

- Onboarding gains an eighth page: Arrival. The final gesture opens your first atmosphere, Rain Drift with warm Nest light, and lands you in Sound Atmosphere inside a living field.
- Landing on Sound Atmosphere keeps the play and pause control in plain sight, so letting the field go is obvious from the first second, no separate stop to learn.
- Nothing autoplays: the arrival begins only because you choose it, and Skip still exits in silence.
- No permission is requested during arrival, the light renders through SonoLune's in-app field, and extending it system-wide remains a later, deliberate choice.
- Fixed onboarding completion never being recorded, which quietly held back gentle-engagement eligibility for every new user.

## Soundsoft Lab Sessions

- The Sessions branch is ready: crafted atmospheres will arrive from Soundsoft Lab over the air, verified layer by layer, and stay for quiet offline listening, the first sessions are coming soon.
- Every supporter session can keep one layer open for everyone, always, tap to hear it in full, hold to open the rest.
- Non-supporters download only the open layers, so nothing is received that cannot be heard.
- Session caches moved to durable app storage, a received atmosphere no longer risks quiet eviction from temporary space.
- Support opens every layer of every session as they arrive.

## Quiet Invitations

- The invitation service, one scheduled notification at most, days of silence between, nothing sent on days you already visited, has graduated out of Labs.
- After a few meaningful days, Mind offers it exactly once: tap to allow, long-press to keep the silence. Either answer is final; the offer never repeats.
- Labs keeps the finer per-category controls for Night, Focus, reflection, and gentle-return invitations.

## Moon Cycle

- The Connect share card gains a Moon Cycle mode: one synodic month of presence, nights entered, focus held, days returned, with no streaks to lose and no graphs to fall behind on.
- The recap poster renders tonight's actual moon phase with true terminator geometry, from new crescent to full, over a whisper of earthshine.
- Mind offers the recap once per cycle, only when the cycle held real presence; letting it pass costs nothing and the next moon brings a fresh one.
- Signature Ember and Aurora finishes remain part of the supporter field in both card modes.
- "Shape the future" and "Report a disturbance" cards now carry intent badges, "We're listening" and "Helps us improve", so their purpose reads at a glance for anyone new to the page.

## Lune Guard

- Fixed Outside mode firing cues when the device screen is off or not interactive.
- A PowerManager.isInteractive guard now skips the cue and reschedules from the original next-fire time.
- Added a screen-on receiver that restarts the Lune Guard interval from the moment the user returns to the screen.
- Both Breath Cue overlay and Quiet Return notification are covered by the same guard.

## Arc Suite

- Every arc now follows one rule: it activates only when you tap its notification. Night no longer auto-applies its filter from the lock screen or background; it waits for the tap like Light, Sound, Focus, and Mind.
- Fixed pending arc notifications launching SonoLune when you simply swiped away the lock screen (seen on Nothing OS / Android 16). Only the Wake alarm now uses a full-screen intent; every other arc opens solely on a deliberate tap.
- Wake is the single exception and keeps its automatic behavior, because an alarm is expected to fire on its own.
- A scheduled night that is never opened no longer resets your light at its end time, Night-End restores only when a night is actually active.
- The notification is now the sole trigger, so it is never redundant and the behavior is identical across screen-on, screen-off, lock screen, background, foreground, and cold start.
- Fixed arc notifications disappearing when the user adds another arc via Add Ritual while a previous arc is waiting in the shade.
- Atmosphere Arc activates on tap like every other arc: tapping its notification plays the scheduled soundscape, preset or saved soundscape, immediately in Sound Atmosphere, rather than loading paused and waiting for a second tap.
- Sound Atmosphere now auto-expands and scrolls to lazy-loaded presets such as Lucid Focus and Sleep Descent when they are the active preset on landing.

## Mind Suite

- Labs pulse animation now uses ValueNotifier so only the small labs subtree rebuilds on each 2-second tick instead of the full suite.
- Landscape permission dialogs are now scrollable and no longer clip content on reduced viewport heights.
- Text is comfortable to read at night, body text, moment subtitles, session-complete typewriter, and button labels all lift to a readable contrast when the night overlay is active.
- The "Shape SonoLune with us" chip now leads directly to Connect, where change is made.

## Light and Wake Arc Polish

- Light Arc landing for saved custom presets such as Afterglow now scrolls the preset strip and highlights the correct card.
- Wake Arc Snooze and Breathe badge row is now always accessible regardless of screen width, text scale, or display resolution.
- The Atmosphere Depth Beta chip now wraps to a second line instead of overflowing the card boundary on narrow screens.
- Play Store update dialog now appears on devices where Play Core reported available but returned a zero version code.

## Steadier Ground

- Every sound layer loops without a click, short clips that previously snapped at the loop point now crossfade tail to head over 400 ms, so the field holds smoothly regardless of clip length.
- Ambient sound is a true background companion now: the audio field is held by a quiet foreground presence, so it continues through a locked screen or an app switch instead of being stopped by the system, carried on the same single field summary you already see, with no second notification beside it.
- Restoring support no longer slips, a genuine restore is always recognized, even when it follows a cancelled purchase, so a returning supporter is never asked to give twice.
- The support page can no longer strand itself: if the store is slow or unreachable it settles into a clear, usable state rather than an endless "Checking support status…".
- Focus sessions always come to rest, the session engine releases cleanly every time, so a finished session can never quietly keep running.
- Scheduled rest keeps honest time: a snoozed wake is never re-armed into the past, so it cannot ring the instant it is rescheduled.
- Your streak and the gentle rhythms SonoLune learns, your preferred minutes by the hour, now survive a full restart instead of resetting to zero.
- The dim control keeps syncing with your screen after a brightness-permission prompt, instead of locking until the next launch.
- Tapping the Light tile from Quick Settings is steady even when SonoLune was fully closed.

## Quieter by Design

- The light field is written once per change instead of twice, steadier overlays and less work for your device.
- Still screens stay still: Experiments, Future, the Lune Guard suite, the ritual hero, and the Focus Flow view all stop redrawing when nothing is moving.
- Quiet invitations no longer recompute on every small change, they settle, and stir only when something truly shifts.
- The moon wallpaper paints lighter during motion, and downloaded sessions verify themselves off to the side so the app never stutters while checking them.
- Textures and the opening mark are decoded only as large as they are shown, so SonoLune starts a touch faster and holds less in memory.

## Nothing Left Behind

- Across a long evening SonoLune lets go of what it no longer needs: Lune Guard break cues, shared field cards, save dialogs, and the focus engine each release their hold rather than quietly accumulating.
- A partially received session no longer leaves a fragment behind when a download is interrupted.

## Calm in the Small Things

- The Lune Guard reset counts all the way to zero before it completes, for its full, honest duration.
- The very first subliminal cue is gentle by default, a soft, brief flash rather than a full-strength one.
- Adding or removing a single micro-texture, birds, leaves, distant thunder, now registers as a real change to a remembered atmosphere.

## Focus Reflection

- When a reflection is saved on the focus completion screen, the writing space settles into quiet confirmation, "Held.", so the moment of acknowledgment is felt, not rushed past.
- "Resting with the others." places the reflection in the same field as every thought you have carried here, with no extra ceremony.
- A single calm invitation, Return to your reflections, opens Mind Suite with your reflections already in view, so nothing needs to be searched for after a session ends.
- The return path appears only after a reflection is saved, so the completion screen stays clear for sessions where you rest without writing.
