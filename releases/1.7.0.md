# SonoLune 1.7.0

**Atmosphere Depth**

The next major Sound Atmosphere branch: a single SonoLune depth field that shapes warmth, room space, layer presence, saved soundscape memory, and Play-safe release prompting without changing the calm defaults.

## Highlights

- Atmosphere Depth becomes a live depth field inside Sound Atmosphere
- Depth stays neutral at zero so existing soundscapes keep their current sound
- The depth field makes Sound Atmosphere feel wider, warmer, and more dimensional without changing the neutral default
- The Sound Atmosphere UI shows Warmth, Space, and Layer presence as readable depth signals instead of separate technical sliders
- Dedicated reverb joins the depth path as a bounded room-space send, while compressor, saturation, and delay remain intentionally skipped
- NeuroLayers gain a companion depth path with subtle widening and tone balance
- Saved soundscapes preserve their chosen depth for later return, Focus Ambient Field, and Lunar Arc use
- Sound Atmosphere layers now crossfade their loop tail into the next head for smoother long listening
- Atmosphere Depth is visible to free users with a clear Supporter gate instead of disappearing from the suite
- Update prompts now wait for Google Play to report a live newer build instead of trusting the website feed
- Wake Arc snooze now keeps one active one-shot alarm and updates the Wake card correctly
- Wake Check now opens as its own small preset sheet from Breath, Light, or Stand chips
- Mind Suite entry is guarded against first-scroll animation glitches when Android animation scale is enabled
- Lunar Arc day mode uses a warmer field for clearer top-section text
- Timed Light Arcs now fire silently without a transient ramp overlay, keeping Night and Focus authority untouched
- Focus setup now returns to the hard top for duration guidance and dismisses the keyboard before preset sheets open
- Returning to a previous suite now preserves main suite scroll positions instead of jumping to the top
- Landscape permission sheets, support thank-you, and Lunar Arc template picking now stay reachable above the SonoLune nav

## Sound Atmosphere

- Added Atmosphere Depth as a supporter depth control inside Sound Atmosphere.
- Depth maps one slider into restrained per-layer balancing, real Android per-session EQ, and a subtle dedicated reverb send instead of exposing technical audio controls.
- The public UI keeps one Atmosphere Depth gesture and shows Warmth, Space, and Layer presence as small readable signals beneath it.
- At zero depth, every depth multiplier is neutral and the EQ path is released so existing mixes are unchanged.
- Higher depth now feels wider and more open while adding stronger warmth, room space, and calmer high edges.
- Atmosphere Depth remains visible to free users, with Supporter opening the full live depth field.
- Depth helper text now stays in SonoLune language instead of exposing technical EQ or reverb controls.
- Local Sound Atmosphere layers now use a guarded tail-to-head loop crossfade instead of hard MediaPlayer loop resets.

## Depth Field

- Android layer playback now receives an atmosphereDepth field and applies a safe depth gain map per layer.
- A per-layer Equalizer attaches only while depth is above zero, then releases when depth returns to neutral or the layer stops.
- A dedicated PresetReverb aux send attaches only while depth is above zero, with larger room space at the high end and cleanup when the field stops.
- Remote Atmosphere layers participate in the same depth field instead of sitting outside the processor.
- Binaural and tone layers receive subtle depth-linked widening and tone balance when NeuroLayers are active.
- Layer gain shaping was adjusted so maximum depth keeps more presence instead of pulling the field too far back.
- The dedicated reverb and native EQ remain separated internally while the user sees one calm depth control.
- Compressor, saturation, and delay are intentionally not part of this release to avoid pumping, clipping, and loop timing artifacts.

## Soundscape Memory

- Saved soundscapes now remember Atmosphere Depth alongside layer mix, NeuroLayers, stereo width, and drift.
- Custom soundscape names can reflect the depth field so shaped sessions feel easier to find later.
- Focus Ambient Field and Lunar Arc handoffs can return to a saved atmosphere with its chosen depth intact.
- Remote Soundsoft Lab fields now read as part of the same Sound Atmosphere depth surface.
- The Sound Atmosphere field reading has been tightened so active layers, depth, and remote sessions feel like one suite.

## Release and Arc Reliability

- Update prompts now use Google Play in-app update availability, so SonoLune does not announce a website release before Play has approved it.
- The website update feed remains available for the Field Journal and full release archive, but no longer controls whether the app prompts an update.
- Wake Arc snooze now replaces any existing snooze for that wake instead of stacking multiple one-shot alarms.
- Expired snoozes are pruned from the active Wake card state, and snooze time labels follow the device clock format.
- Wake Check chips now open a dedicated preset sheet for Breath, Light, and Stand instead of routing through the full Wake editor.
- Launcher shortcuts now show dedicated Focus-active and Night-active guidance instead of scheduled-arc snackbar copy.
- Mind Suite waits for its entry animation before accepting first scroll, preventing repeated glass/text rows under Android animation scale.
- Focus now teaches custom minutes with a one-time long-press hint that hides permanently after the user discovers it.
- Focus duration validation now scrolls to the hard top setup area, and Settle/preset pickers dismiss the custom-duration keyboard.
- Lunar Arc day field contrast was warmed so the hero text remains readable in bright afternoon mode.
- Timed Light Arcs now fire silently without a transient ramp overlay, preventing stuck visual fields during active Night or Focus sessions.
- Sound Atmosphere held mode keeps the suspended field feeling dimmed without drawing a thin edge outline around the page.
- The shell now remembers main suite scroll positions across Light, Mind, Lune Field, Lune Guard, Lunar Arc, Night, Focus, and Sound returns.
- Sound Atmosphere remembers expanded Soundscapes, Micro Textures, Neuro Layers, Atmosphere Depth, and Drift Duration between navigation and cold starts.
- Focus permission sheets in landscape lift above the in-app navigation and scroll when needed.
- The support thank-you page and Lunar Arc template picker now remain scrollable/reachable in landscape.
