# SonoLune 1.9.4

**Focus, made a calm ritual, name the work, choose the room, and finish without being chased to the next session** · 24 Jun 2026

*An Insider build: it went to testers and never reached the public store.*

This release reimagines the Focus session as a calm, considered ritual rather than a bare timer. You begin by naming what you are sitting down to do, and that thread is read back to you at the close, a session with a real beginning and end. You can choose a "room": one of a handful of Focus atmospheres, Violet Field, Warm Study, Deep Harbor, Paper Dawn, Clear Glass, that retint the whole running screen as one coherent mood and can match your Calm Light and Ambient Field with a single tap (Violet Field is the familiar look, so nothing changes until you choose), and matching the room brings its own air, a fire crackling low in the warm rooms or far water in Deep Harbor. If you use Focus Flow, its breathing background takes the room's colour too, and an optional soft chime can mark the moment you begin and the moment you finish. An optional settling breath opens a fresh session with a few slow breaths before the timer starts; an optional soft companion light breathes beside you as you work, so the room never feels empty. Finishing is a slow dawn rather than a snap, and the old streak-pressure is gone, replaced by a simple "that session is complete; nothing else is owed." Smaller refinements run all the way through: a paused session reads as gently held rather than stalled, the duration chips spring under your finger, the reflection field became a warm place to write, and you can quietly check off each thread as you go. Everything new is opt-in or defaults to exactly the previous look, so an untouched Focus session is unchanged. Alongside Focus, this build also steadies every screen at the largest system fonts and display sizes, and brings a wave of quiet refinements to Light, Presence, Sound, the Lunar Arc, and Night.

- Choose the room: a small set of Focus atmospheres, Violet Field, Warm Study, Deep Harbor, Paper Dawn, Clear Glass, retint the whole running screen as one coherent mood, and can match your Calm Light and Ambient Field with a single tap. Violet Field is the familiar look, so nothing changes unless you choose it.
- A room you can hear: matching the light and sound to an atmosphere now brings that room its own air, a fire crackling low in Warm Study and Paper Dawn, far water in Deep Harbor, a clean hush in Clear Glass.
- A sound to begin and end by: an optional soft, warm chime can mark the moment you start a session and the moment you finish, a quiet bell at the threshold that swells gently in and resolves rather than cutting off, never sounding during the work itself.
- Whispers in the room's colour: if you use Focus Flow, its slow breathing background now takes a gentle wash of your chosen atmosphere's colour too.
- A breath before you begin: turn on Settling breath and a fresh session opens with a few slow breaths over a warm, breathing screen before the timer starts, a threshold to cross, always skippable with a tap.
- Quiet company: an optional soft light breathes beside the timer while you work, with a gentle word now and then, so the room never feels empty.
- A kinder close: finishing is now a slow dawn rather than a snap, and the old "keep your streak going" nudge is gone, replaced by "That session is complete. Nothing else is owed."
- A gentle close to the loop: when you named a thread, the end quietly asks whether you stayed with it, "Stayed with it" or "Drifted, that's okay", never a judgement, and a single calm line notes how many sessions you have held this week, with no targets or streaks to defend.

## A session with a beginning and an end

- Name what you are sitting down to do as you begin; it carries into the running screen and is read back to you at the close.
- At the end, a gentle "did you stay with your thread?", Stayed with it, or Drifted (which is explicitly okay), saved with your reflection, never a score.
- Finishing is a slow dawn rather than a snap, the streak no longer pressures the next session, and a single calm line notes how many you have held this week, no targets, no streak to defend.
- A paused session reads as gently held; the reflection at the end rests in a warm, considered panel.
- In the evening, once a session is genuinely set for the next day, a quiet line anticipates it, "Your Focus space opens again tomorrow morning", gentle and pressure-free, and never shown during the day.

## Choose the room

- Pick a Focus atmosphere, Violet Field, Warm Study, Deep Harbor, Paper Dawn, Clear Glass, and the whole running screen (timer glow, progress, the close) takes that one mood.
- One tap can match your Calm Light and Ambient Field to the room, and brings the room its own air: a low fire in the warm rooms, far water in Deep Harbor, a clean hush in Clear Glass. It never changes them on its own.
- If you use Focus Flow, its breathing background takes the room colour too. Violet Field is the look you already know, so the default is unchanged.
- A plain-language line under "Choose the room" now says what a room is for: it recolours this Focus screen, tap to preview, then Match light & sound to carry the mood across.

## Quiet rituals around the work

- Settling breath opens a fresh session with a few slow breaths before the timer starts, pure arrival, skippable with a tap.
- Quiet company keeps a soft light breathing beside the timer, with a gentle word now and then.
- An optional soft chime can mark the two thresholds, swelling in as you begin and resolving as you finish, never sounding during the work itself.
- All are opt-in and off until you choose them.

## Comfortable at every text and screen size

- Stress-tested at the largest system font, the largest display size and bold text: titles, status lines and labels now read in full instead of trailing into "…". The Lune Guard title, the Focus "match light & sound to this room" button, the suite names along the bottom bar, and the ongoing notification summary all hold their whole text, wrapping to a second line when they need the room.
- The horizontal card strips, Sound soundscapes, the Focus rooms, the Light suite's Colour Veil presets, and both Mind moment rails, now melt softly at both edges instead of being sliced at the screen edge, so the last card always invites a scroll. The Focus timer presets re-flow to a second line rather than clipping, and the Mind moment tiles (and their rail) grow with your font so their titles stay whole.
- In landscape, the snooze and wake sheets, the breath flow and the Mind Programmer all scroll properly, so a Start or Resume button is never stranded below the fold.
- More surfaces held to the same standard: the Lune Guard "no break logged" status, the Mind Programmer title, the Lunar Arc header (its corner moon now lifts clear of the title), and the Today-Arc cards, names, preview lines and the "Flowing" status, all read in full at the largest fonts instead of dotting.
- Night now fades its scrolling content softly into the dark at the top and bottom, melting at the edges rather than clipping hard, and the last card clears the "pull down / hold to exit" hint pinned at the bottom.
- All cosmetic, nothing about how Focus, Sound, Mind, Light, the Arc or Night behaves has changed; the screens simply hold together at sizes they used to strain at.

## Light and Presence, refined

- Once you allow system-wide light, the "apply everywhere" badge now stays in place and reads back what the field is carrying across your apps, warmth, dim, matte and grain, a colour veil, the living field, updating live as you tune, instead of vanishing and leaving a gap.
- The Paper Grain toggle now lays down a faint grain on its own, even with the Paper Matte slider at rest, so switching it on always changes something.
- Presence Field now names what it is right beneath the title, a visual detox, a field rather than a fence, SonoLune's calm answer to app blockers.
- When the Presence Field is settled over your screen, nudging a Light control, or a Space or Living Moment in Mind, now answers with a gentle "the field is settled; lift it from the Field tab to tune," rather than letting two systems quietly fight over the same screen.
- The Presence Field's evening wind-down now lands you consistently on the Night room in its calm, pre-active state, ready to begin Night when you choose, instead of sometimes dropping you straight into Night, sometimes half-in, and sometimes not at all.
- Smaller touches: the warm-light readout reads as a clean candlelight range, the moon in the Sound header always clears the title at large fonts, and the "Light Across Surfaces" cards hold their labels so "Color Content" no longer spills past its edge.
