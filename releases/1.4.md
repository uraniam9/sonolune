# SonoLune 1.4

**The deepest evolution of your atmosphere yet** · 30 Apr 2026

A quieter, more aware SonoLune begins to take shape, where light, sound, and presence move together with you.

## Highlights

- Quiet Veil, Paper Matte control, Colour Veil, and custom Light Suite presets
- Living Field is now part of Light Suite and anchors the final 30 seconds of Focus with a light ramp
- Living Moments, dynamic suggestions, Quiet Invitations, and Share your Field
- Ambient System, Accessibility coverage, Field Journal, and Lune Field wallpaper
- Night exit refined with anchors, long-press guidance, and daylight animation
- Notification summary refined for theme-safe readability and practical field depth

## Light Suite Evolves

- Welcome Quiet Veil and advanced tuning in Light Suite.
- Paper Matte control now includes an adjustable slider so surface texture can be shaped to your eyes.
- Colour Veil brings an RGB mixer for moving through infinite shades and tuning light to emotion.
- Create and save your own presets so your perfect atmosphere is always easy to return to.
- Living Field now belongs inside Light Suite, including the final 30 second Focus light ramp for free users and supporters.

## Presence and Connection

- Share your Field turns your current atmosphere into a post with three expressions from Connect.
- SonoLune Connect page was updated to better align with the current field, journal, and sharing surfaces.
- Dynamic moment suggestions now gently fit time and state more intelligently.
- Quiet Invitations arrived in Labs as an experimental way to invite others into shared stillness.
- Living Moments joined Mind Suite as scenes that breathe, evolve, and reflect inner state.

## Atmosphere Deepens

- Ambient System was introduced as a flowing layer that adapts with you.
- The foundation for atmosphere depth in Sound Atmosphere is now in place for richer sonic immersion.
- After Sunset Queue toggle helps the night flow with more intention.
- Moon Haze now lives inside Night Environment as an explicit night-only veil, off by default and applied only from Night itself.
- Exit from Night Environment is optimized with anchors, helper text, and a daylight animation that makes returning to day feel intentional.

## Focus, Clarity, Guidance

- Focus Suite now includes a more practical guide helper for real-world use of sessions.
- Greyscale toggle was improved for a calmer, less distracting field.

## System and Structure

- Accessibility permission arrived in Labs to enable fuller system-wide light coverage.
- SonoLune now carries a complete Field Journal of public releases from v1.0 onward.
- Notification summary now stays readable across Android light and dark themes, with safer compact and expanded layouts.
- Field depth and Night density now use more practical, shaped calculations instead of raw intensity values.
- Support page and support gate were updated to align with the current SonoLune version and suite language.
- Track the journey and see the evolution of the field over time.

## Lune Field Wallpaper

- Lune Field extends Night Environment into a live dynamic wallpaper.
- Customize sky, parallax, moon position, moon lane, moon size, moon shape, horizon, star density, moon glow, and depth.
- Endless combinations, with a stronger sense of immersion.

## And Beyond

- Performance improvements across the experience.
- Bug fixes and smaller refinements throughout SonoLune.
- Some changes are felt more than listed.
