# SonoLune 1.9.2

**Presence Field, app by app, choose where it lives, let it settle as you rest, and let the screen gently let go**

*An Insider build: it went to testers and never reached the public store.*

Presence Field can now meet you app by app. Choose the few apps you want it for and the field appears only there, every other screen stays untouched. You decide how long you may use a chosen app freely, from instantly to half an hour, before the field begins to settle; then it eases in slowly, the colour draining and the screen softening toward hardly-visible, so the app grows genuinely restful to leave. It stays tap-through and escapable throughout, and lifts the moment you touch anything or move on. When the screen settles, a soft invitation now floats over the app itself, one tap slips you into Night in the evening, or a Focus by day, and, if you like, a warm wind-down sound can anchor the moment, even in silence. A short, gentle walkthrough opens the first time you visit, so the field explains itself from the very first moment. These per-app touches are the one place SonoLune asks for Usage Access, requested only there, used only to tell which app is in front, never to read your screen, and with no accessibility service. Everything stays optional, off until you choose it, and a single tap still lifts the whole field. This release also makes SonoLune clearer to live with: every suite now says plainly what it is for, a new Help & Guides hub gathers a gentle walkthrough for each, the quiet you settle into gains a living grain and an optional warm chime to let go by, and the app itself opens faster, its moon waking with a warm, breathing glow.

## The field, app by app

- Pick the few apps you want the field for; it appears only there, with a one-to-two-second settle as you switch.
- Foreground identity comes from Usage Access (which app is in front), never your screen content, and no accessibility service.
- When you go to grant Usage Access, a coach card floats over the settings list and points you to the one switch, the same gentle guide SonoLune uses for its other permissions.
- Leave the list empty and the field stays global, exactly as before.

## Rest, and release

- Choose how long a chosen app stays free, from instantly to thirty minutes, before the field begins; then it eases in slowly, never a sudden change.
- As you linger, the quiet deepens, the colour draining, the screen softening toward hardly-visible, always tap-through, and lifted at the first touch.
- When it settles, a soft invitation floats over the app itself: tap to slip into Night in the evening, or a Focus by day.
- An optional wind-down sound can anchor the moment with a soft, warm swell, even in silence, or your own atmosphere swells back in.
- Everything is optional and off until you choose it; one tap lifts the whole field.

## Clear from the first moment

- A short, gentle walkthrough opens the first time you enter the room, so the Presence Field explains itself before you have to wonder, and it stays a tap away whenever you want it again.
- A plainer line under the name tells you at a glance what it is for, and the room now wears SonoLune's signature with warmer, softer surfaces throughout.

- Mind moves to the still center of the bar, the place you return to. Light (dawn) and Night (dusk) become the two outer bookends, with Focus and Field gathered on either side of Mind.
- A gentle horizontal swipe now carries you to the neighbouring suite with a soft directional slide; tapping a tab travels the same way, so the whole app moves as one continuous motion.
- The centered Mind tab carries a quiet home glow and a slightly fuller form, so the center is always easy to find without a hard highlight.

## System presence, in the open

- The System Presence card in Mind now rests as a calm constellation of soft dots, one for each of the seven accesses SonoLune can use (Light, Notifications, Brightness, Do Not Disturb, Exact alarm, Accessibility, and Usage), where it once showed only four, so it tells you the state at a glance without a tall stack of rows.
- Tap "Details" and the full list unfolds, each access carrying a small tag for the suite it serves, Core, Summaries, Dim, Focus, Lunar Arc, Coverage, Field, then folds quietly away again.
- Each row opens gently to explain, in plain words, why and where that access is felt, nothing is ever a mystery.
- Only Light is core. A calm amber line notes when the core light field is ready, while the green "fully enabled" is kept for when all seven are granted.
- The honest promise stays: permissions appear only when you reach a feature that needs them, and SonoLune never requests background or unused access, and the developer page now lists the per-app Usage Access alongside the rest.
