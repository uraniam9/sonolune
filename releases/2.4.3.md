# SonoLune 2.4.3

**The light that would not come back, and three refusals that never said so** · 13 Sep 2026

A small release on top of a large one. If you are coming from 2.4.1 or earlier, Autumn is still new to you: a room of dry wind, cicada haze and falling leaves, free and first on the shelf, and the three sounds that arrived with it - Salt Air, Evening Pond and Cicadas. Lay cicadas over leaves and the field names the season. Everything else here is repair. Tapping the bulb in your notification shade, or the Quick Settings tile, put the light out correctly and then would not put it back - unless SonoLune itself happened to be the app on screen. Both taps take the same path, so the tap was never the difference. Taking the light away has nothing standing in front of it; putting it back passes nine checks, and one of those is held open for the whole time the app is in the background. That check exists for a good reason: it stops the light being added while SonoLune cannot yet tell which app you have moved to, and it closes the moment it can name one. On a phone that has never been given usage access it can never name one, so it stayed open for the rest of the session and every request for the light back was refused in silence - a scheduled Night included. Opening SonoLune closed it, which is exactly why opening the app was the only thing that ever helped. The Night invitation had the same shape underneath. It arrives at the hour you choose, and before it speaks it asks whether you are already in the room it is offering, because an invitation into Night that arrives during Night is noise. The answer it was reading is written only when SonoLune is running, and this is a notification that wakes when the app is not - so after an evening in Night the reading froze at "you are in Night" and stayed frozen for as long as the app did not run again. Which, on the evenings an invitation exists for, is all of them. It fired in the daytime because opening the app during the day wrote a fresh answer over the stale one. A reading nobody has refreshed for six hours is now treated as not knowing, and not knowing has never been a reason to take something away from you. The same door can now tell you when it deliberately stayed quiet, in plain words, in the app you actually have - because a door held back on purpose and a door that never fired have looked identical from outside for three releases. In Mind, a long press on the moon did nothing at all while Night was running. Holding your space during Night is genuinely refused, and rightly: holding it takes the light to nothing, and Night IS a light state, so undoing it quietly is the one thing that must not happen. But the same line also refused the opposite request. Coming BACK to a space you had already held was blocked too - and entering Night does not release a held space, so that is an ordinary evening, and the app tells you in six places, two of them notifications that arrive while it is closed, that the moon is how you return. A control may be stopped from making things worse. It may never be stopped from being undone. The return is answered first now, and the refusal that remains says what it is and how to clear it. And on the Sound atmosphere cards, "Hold to preview" was cut off mid-word. The two hints shared the row by splitting it in half before either of them was measured, and the shorter one held the half with room to spare while the longer one was trimmed to fit. They ask for the width they need now, and if a card is ever too narrow for both they shrink together rather than losing a word.

## Highlights

- Autumn and three new sounds arrived in the last release, and if you are coming from 2.4.1 or earlier they are new to you. Autumn is a room made of dry wind, cicada haze and falling leaves, free, and first on the Soundscapes shelf. Salt Air is an open coast with the sea underneath it. Evening Pond is still fresh water at dusk - the first still water this app has ever had, because every water in it was either falling or moving. And Cicadas is a high unbroken haze that joins Birds and Leaves as a third micro texture. Lay cicadas over leaves and the field names the season rather than a place: Autumn Wind, Autumn Forest, or on its own, the Turning Year. All three are free, and each says so until the first time you turn it on.
- The light comes back from the bulb and the tile, wherever you are. Switching it off from the shade always worked; switching it back on only worked while SonoLune was the app on screen, and there was nothing on the phone to say why. The two halves are not symmetrical in the code - taking the light away answers immediately, and putting it back asks nine questions first - so a fault could live in one direction for a long time without touching the other.
- The Night invitation reaches you at night. It checks, before it speaks, whether you are already in Night - which is right, because an invitation into a room you are standing in is noise, and noise gets a whole channel muted. But that answer is only written while SonoLune is running, and an invitation wakes when it is not. After one evening in Night the answer froze at "you are in Night" and stayed there until the app next ran, so the invitation was swallowed on exactly the evenings it exists for: the ones where you have been away. It arrived in the daytime because opening the app in the daytime wrote a fresh answer over it. An answer older than six hours is now treated as not knowing, and not knowing has never been a reason to take something away.
- The light one was a question held open and never closed. While you are away from SonoLune the light waits rather than settling somewhere it cannot yet identify, and that wait ends as soon as the app can name the screen you moved to. A phone that has never granted usage access cannot name it, ever - so the wait never ended, and everything that asked for the light back was answered with nothing. A scheduled Night arriving while the phone sat on the home screen was refused the same way. Waiting for an answer is now allowed to end, and with nothing on screen to protect there is no longer any reason to withhold.
- And a door that stays quiet on purpose can now say so. The app has recorded every one of these deliberate silences since the day the feature was written, and nothing has ever read that record - so a door aimed correctly and swallowed every night looked exactly like a door that never fired, while the panel went on promising the next one. The doors panel now says, in plain words, when the last one stayed quiet and what had the screen.
- The moon in Mind can bring your space back during Night. Holding your space while Night is running is still refused - holding it takes the light to nothing, and Night is a light state, so undoing that in silence is the one thing this app must not do. It says so now instead of doing nothing. The return, though, was being refused by the same line, and that is a dead end: entering Night leaves a held space held, so it is an ordinary evening, and the app tells you in six places that the moon is the way back. A control may be stopped from making things worse. It may never be stopped from being undone.
- A hint on the Sound cards was cut off mid-word. "Hold to preview" read as "Hold to previ...", because the two hints in that little bar split the row down the middle before either had been measured - so the shorter one sat in its half with room left over while the longer one was trimmed to fit. Not a large-font problem: the card grows with the font, so it was worst at the ordinary size. They take the width they ask for now.
- The thirty-second preview on the deeper sound controls now says something at the end of it. Reach for one of those controls and SonoLune opens it free for half a minute before anybody mentions money, and when that closes it asks whether you want to keep it. The asking only happened if you were still watching the Sound screen - so the ordinary ending, where you set the field and put the phone down, was that you heard the thing, the free preview was counted as used for the day, and nobody said a word. The ask now waits and arrives the next time you open Sound. It stays away while Night is holding the phone, stays away between ten at night and seven in the morning, and is dropped altogether after a day. It is still one preview per control per day; this is the sentence that was owed at the end of it, not a second window.

## The light that would not come back on

- The bulb in the notification shade and the Quick Settings tile both put the light out correctly and then refused to bring it back, unless SonoLune was the app in front of you at the time.
- The two directions are not symmetrical. Taking the light away answers straight away. Putting it back asks nine questions first, and exactly one of them depends on whether you are inside the app.
- That one is a wait, not a refusal: while you are away, the light holds rather than settling over a screen SonoLune cannot yet identify. The wait is meant to end as soon as it can name the app you moved to.
- On a phone that has never granted usage access, it can never name one. So the wait never ended, and every request for the light back was withheld for the rest of the session - including a scheduled Night arriving while the phone sat idle.
- Opening SonoLune ended the wait, which is why opening the app was the only thing that appeared to help.
- Waiting long enough is now treated as an answer. And when there is nothing on screen to protect, a request for the light is never withheld - there is no earlier judgement to keep and nothing to flicker.
- A side effect worth having: the check behind this was also polling three times a second, indefinitely, arguing with itself. It rests now.

## An invitation that could not arrive at night

- The Night invitation fires at the hour you choose. Before it speaks it asks whether you are already in Night, because arriving into the room it is offering is noise - and noise gets a whole channel muted, which costs the invitations that were working.
- The answer it reads is written only while SonoLune is running. An invitation wakes when it is not. So after an evening in Night the answer froze at "you are in Night", and stayed frozen until the app next ran.
- That is every evening the invitation exists for. These only speak on days you have been away - which are precisely the days nothing had run to write a fresh answer.
- It arrived during the day because opening SonoLune in the daytime wrote a fresh answer over the stale one. That is the whole of "it only fires in the day".
- Focus and the wake reminder were never affected: neither of them asks about Night, and a focus session ends while the app is still running to write it down.
- An answer older than six hours is now treated as not knowing. A Night you are genuinely sitting in still silences the invitation, exactly as before.

## A door that can say it stayed quiet

- Every time one of these doors holds itself back, it writes down which one and why. It has done so since the day the feature was written, and nothing in the app has ever read it.
- So a door aimed correctly and swallowed every single night looked identical to a door that never fired, while the settings panel went on saying when the next one was due. Three releases have now been spent inside that silence.
- The doors panel says it plainly now, in the app everybody has rather than only in a development build: when the last one stayed quiet, and what had the screen at the time.
- If you see that line, the door is working and the hour is simply wrong for you - your Night starts before the invitation does. Move the invitation earlier.

## The moon that would not let you back in

- A long press on the moon in Mind did nothing at all while Night was running. Not holding your space, not returning to it, and no word either way.
- Refusing to hold it is right. Holding your space takes the light to nothing, and Night is a light state - undoing it quietly is the one thing that must not happen. It now says so, in the same words every other thing Night holds already uses.
- Refusing the return was a dead end. Entering Night leaves a space that was already held still held, so it is an ordinary evening - and the app tells you in six places, two of them notifications that arrive while it is closed, that the moon is how you come back.
- A control may be stopped from making things worse. It may never be stopped from being undone. The return is answered first now.
- Coming back during Night hands your light to where Night gives it back at the end, rather than painting it over a night in progress. The space returns whole when the night ends, and Night is untouched until then.

## A hint with its tail cut off

- On the Sound atmosphere cards, "Hold to preview" read as "Hold to previ...".
- The little bar holds two hints, and they were sharing the row by splitting it in half - a split decided before either of them had been measured. The shorter hint held the half it did not need, and the longer one was trimmed to fit the half it could not.
- The gap in the middle was not spacing. It was the width the second hint needed and could not have.
- Not a large-font fault, which is why the large-font sweep never caught it: the card grows with the system font, so both sides grow together and the halving bit hardest at the ordinary size.
- Each hint now takes the width it asks for, and if a card is ever too narrow for both they shrink together rather than one of them losing a word.

## The offer that was spent in silence

- Reach for one of the deeper sound controls without having supported the app, and SonoLune opens it for thirty seconds first - free, no dialog, no asking - so you hear the thing itself before anybody mentions money. When the thirty seconds end, it asks.
- That ask only arrived if you were still looking at the Sound screen. Which is the one thing this app is built for you not to do: set the field, put the phone down, sleep.
- So the ordinary ending was that you heard the deeper field, the free preview for that control was counted as used for the day, and nothing was ever said to you. The preview was spent on somebody who was never asked.
- The ask is now written down when the window closes, rather than spoken into a room you have left, and it arrives the next time you open Sound.
- It is held back in the places it has no business appearing: never while Night is holding the phone, never between ten at night and seven in the morning whatever the mode says, and never to somebody who has since supported the app. Somebody opening Sound at three in the morning is reaching for rain because they cannot sleep, and that is not a moment to ask anyone for anything.
- A wrong hour does not cancel it - it waits for a better one. After a day it is dropped entirely, because by then you no longer remember what you heard, and an offer that has to explain itself first is not an offer.
- What has not changed: it is still one free window per control per day, and this is not a second one. The preview was always spent at the moment it opened. This only repairs the sentence that was owed at the end of it.
- Separately, the card at the foot of Mind now says what is actually behind it. "Shape SonoLune with us" is the only permanent way to reach the page where the app is supported, and every word on the card - the title, the line under it, the pill - was about sending in a thought. So somebody who had used SonoLune every night for a month and would happily have contributed had to guess that "Shape" led there. The line now reads "Share a thought, or support the work behind it". The card still opens Connect at the top rather than the shop, and the page still introduces itself before it asks for anything.
