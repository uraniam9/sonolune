# SonoLune 1.1

**Cinematic Night Update** · 28 Feb 2026

The first major refinement after Stable 1, focused on Night, deeper Focus integration, and a stronger home identity.

## Highlights

- Cinematic Night Engine with scheduled night environments
- Deeper Android Focus integration
- Ambient Field added to home
- Moon, splash screen, and icons redesigned

## Release Notes

- Cinematic Night Engine with scheduled night environments.
- Focus suite deeply integrated with the Android system.
- New Focus Environment settings with DND, grayscale, calm light, and ambient sound.

- Redesigned SonoLune Moon, splash screen, and icons.
- Added the new Nest light preset for shared family devices.
- Included UI refinements, stability improvements, and bug fixes.
