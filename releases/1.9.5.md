# SonoLune 1.9.5

**The Light suite, made luxurious, living preset cards, a one-tap Settle, and a field that quietly echoes tonight's moon** · 30 Jun 2026

*An Insider build: it went to testers and never reached the public store.*

This insider build is a premium pass over the Light suite, every surface made warmer, more tactile and more alive, while staying entirely calm. The four presets now breathe: choosing one lets a slow gradient of its own light-character drift behind the card, so Drift, Vellum, Clarity and Nest each preview their mood rather than sitting as flat tiles. A new one-tap Settle rests above the presets, a single calm tap eases the whole suite into a soft, warm, gently dimmed state of its own, with no preset to hunt for. The colour chips come alive too: the tint, ambient-tone and palette swatches you have selected gently sweep their own colours. Vellum now delivers its full reading surface on the first tap, the real e-paper grain, not just the matte slider, warmed by a soft, lamp-lit vignette beneath the grain. The whole panel gains quiet depth: the active preset lifts, and every section card rests on a soft shadow with a faint lit-from-above sheen. A faint moonlit divider marks where the presets give way to the tuning below, and it, along with the settle bloom, glows in tonight's actual moon-phase colour, worked out offline with no network: dim indigo at the new moon, bright silver-blue at the full. Switching a preset settles with a soft bloom rather than a hard cut, leaving Light for Night now eases across as a gentle cross-fade, the ambient fade names its own character, Pulsing, Tidal, Drifting, Becalmed, and a barely-there hearth glow breathes at the screen edges when warmth runs high. Every new motion rests into a tasteful still state when you ask for reduced motion, and nothing here changes what anything does, the Light you know is exactly where you left it, simply warmer to live in.

## Highlights

- Living preset cards: choosing Drift, Vellum, Clarity or Nest now lets a slow gradient of that preset's own light-character drift behind the card, so each active preset previews its mood instead of sitting as a flat tile.
- One-tap Settle: a calm, full-width Settle rests above the presets, a single tap eases the whole suite into its own soft, warm, gently dimmed calm, the quietest softness in the suite. It is its own state, not a preset to find.
- A field that echoes the moon: the moonlit divider and the settle bloom now glow in tonight's real moon-phase colour, dim indigo at the new moon, bright silver-blue at the full, worked out offline, with no network and nothing sent.
- Living colour chips: the tint, ambient-tone and palette swatches you have chosen gently sweep their own colours, so a selected scene feels alive while the rest stay calm and still.
- Vellum, the full reading surface: activating Vellum now lays down its real e-paper grain on the first tap, not only the matte slider, warmed by a soft, lamp-lit vignette beneath the grain.
- Quiet premium depth: the active preset card lifts off the strip, and every section card now rests on a soft shadow with a faint lit-from-above sheen, so the panel reads as tactile material rather than flat rectangles.
- A softer way to change and to leave: switching a preset settles with a gentle bloom rather than a snap, and leaving Light for Night now eases across as a smooth cross-fade.
- The ambient fade, named: the fade interval now reads its own character, Pulsing, Tidal, Drifting, Becalmed, beside the seconds.
- The field, named more softly: the ambient modes now describe their own character, a still field at rest, one colour blooming into the next, the room always gently drifting, so choosing how the light moves reads like a line rather than a setting.
- A hearth at the edges: when warmth runs high, a barely-there warm glow breathes at the screen edges, like firelight just out of sight.
- Calm by default: every new motion rests into a tasteful still state when you turn on reduced motion, and nothing here changes how Light behaves, it is simply warmer and more alive to rest in.

## The presets, brought to life

- The active preset card's surface becomes a slow, living gradient of its own light-character, warm red for Drift, paper-gold for Vellum, clear blue for Clarity, soft accent for Nest, while resting cards stay calm and still.
- Switching presets settles with a soft radial bloom rather than a hard cut, so a change reads as the light arriving.
- The active card lifts on a gentle shadow; resting cards keep a faint one, so the strip reads as tactile chips.

## Settle, in one calm tap

- A full-width Settle rests above the presets: one tap eases the suite into a dedicated calm, gentle warmth, a soft circadian blue-reduction, the suite's most quieting softness, and a light dim.
- It settles into its own state rather than borrowing a preset, so nothing is mislabeled and the bloom marks the moment.

## A field that echoes the sky

- The moonlit divider between the presets and the tuning, and the settle bloom, both take tonight's real moon-phase colour, dim indigo at the new moon, bright silver-blue at the full.
- It is pure offline arithmetic from the same Moon Cycle SonoLune already keeps, no network, nothing sent, so the suite quietly reflects the actual sky.
- Deliberately subtle, and it shifts across the lunar month, so the room is never quite the same two weeks apart.

## Warmer surfaces, gentler motion

- Vellum delivers its full e-paper grain on the first tap, warmed by a soft vignette beneath the texture.
- Section cards rest on a soft shadow with a faint top sheen; the selected colour chips sweep their own hues.
- Leaving Light for Night eases across as a smooth cross-fade, the ambient fade names its own character, and a barely-there hearth glow breathes at the edges when warmth runs high.
- The ambient modes now name their character in plain poetry, a still field at rest, one colour blooming into the next, a room always gently drifting, and the Settle button quietly invites you to "let the light settle."
- Every new motion honours reduced motion, settling into a still, tasteful state.
