# SonoLune 2.0.0

**SonoLune 2.0, a new room for your screen, a Focus you can settle into, and a Light field that can follow your sun** · 25 Jun 2026

This is the biggest SonoLune since launch, the whole arc of work since 1.8.0 gathered into one calm step forward. At the heart of it is Presence Field: an entirely new room beside Night where your sound and your screen settle as one. It is a visual detox, a field, not a fence, that draws a warm, matte quiet over the screen as your atmosphere deepens, eases the apps that pull at you toward hardly-visible the longer you rest in them, and lifts the instant you touch anything. You can give it only the apps you choose, let it ease in when a Focus session ends, or schedule it to meet your evening on its own, never a wall, never a lock, always one tap to lift. Focus, too, is reimagined as a quiet ritual rather than a bare timer: you name what you are sitting down to do and it is read back to you at the close, you can choose a "room" that recolours the whole screen and match it to your light and sound, and finishing is a slow dawn with no streak left to chase. Light goes deeper, a tactile Kindle-paper finish over the matte, a Colour Veil for living scenes, and a screen that can follow your own sun (worked out offline, with no GPS) to ease into night around your local dusk, reaching the very edges of the screen with only the draw-over-apps permission. Night now arrives on your timing, from an instant on to a long, unhurried drift. A Help & Guides hub gathers a gentle walkthrough for every suite, each one now says plainly what it is for, your sound keeps playing when you swipe SonoLune away, and the whole app has been hardened to hold its full text and layout at the largest accessibility fonts. Everything new is optional and off until you choose it, so the SonoLune you know is still exactly where you left it, there is simply a great deal more of it.

## Presence Field, a new room for your screen

- A co-regulated field beside Night: as your Sound Atmosphere deepens, the screen settles with it on one shared axis, a warm, matte quiet drawn over everything. It descends over two to three seconds when it arms and returns just as softly, so you always feel it arrive.
- A visual detox, a field not a fence, the calm answer to app blockers. It gently quiets the apps that pull you in toward hardly-visible the longer you rest in them, stays tap-through and escapable throughout, and lifts the instant you touch anything.
- Give it only the apps you choose, and it appears there and nowhere else. This is the one place SonoLune asks for Usage Access, only to tell which app is in front, never to read your screen.
- It can ease in when a Focus session ends, drift a soft breath cue by while you rest, wash colour toward grey when it feels loud, or lend Night a little more amber. Schedule it through Lunar Arc to meet your evening on its own, and one tap always lifts the whole field.
- You decide how long a chosen app stays free before the quiet begins, from instantly to half an hour, and how long a quick glance away is forgiven before it resets. When the quiet settles, the screen can let go to a deep, grainy hush with a soft invitation to wind down, and an optional felt pulse firms up in your hand as a cue to set the app down.

## Focus, made a calm ritual

- Name what you are sitting down to do as you begin; it is read back to you at the close, a session with a real beginning and end, not just a clock.
- Choose a room, Violet Field, Warm Study, Deep Harbor, Paper Dawn, Clear Glass, that recolours the whole running screen as one mood, and match your Calm Light and Ambient Field to it with a single tap, each room bringing its own air.
- An optional settling breath before the timer, a soft companion light beside you as you work, and a warm chime to mark the start and the close, all off until you choose them.
- Finishing is a slow dawn rather than a snap, and the old "keep your streak going" pressure is gone, "that session is complete; nothing else is owed."

## Light and Night, deepened

- Paper Matte gains a true paper finish, a tactile e-paper grain over the matte, like a Kindle, with a slider to shape how present it feels, plus a Colour Veil tint lane for living scenes.
- A one-tap Settle now rests above the presets, easing the Light suite into a dedicated calm: a gentle warmth, a soft blue-reduction, its most quieting softness and a light dim, marked by a bloom that, like the moonlit divider above the tuning, wears tonight's real moon-phase colour, dim indigo at the new moon and silver-blue at the full (pure offline arithmetic, nothing sent).
- The whole suite gained warmer surfaces: Vellum lays down its full e-paper grain on the first tap, the section cards rest on a soft shadow with a faint sheen, a barely-there hearth glow breathes at the edges when warmth runs high, and leaving Light for Night eases across as a smooth cross-fade. Warm Light follows a true colour-temperature curve now, a gentle warm-white at a touch and deep candlelight at full (the slider shows the temperature as you move it), and Quiet Veil is a clean, tint-free haze, distinct from Paper Matte's warm grain. Every new motion settles into a still state when you ask for reduced motion.
- The screen can follow your own sun: set your rough latitude once (offline, no GPS, nothing sent) and Auto Night follows your local sunset and sunrise, folding into the body-clock curve it already keeps, or leave it off for a simple evening window.
- The Light field reaches the status bar, navigation bar, and notch on its own, using only draw-over-apps, no accessibility needed.
- Night arrives on your timing, from an instant on to a long, unhurried drift, and a corner of Labs holds quiet device-level options like restoring the field after a restart.

## Finding your way

- Mind rests at the still centre of the suite bar, the place you return to, with Light (dawn) and Night (dusk) as the two outer ends of the day and Focus and Field gathered on either side of Mind.
- A gentle horizontal swipe carries you to the neighbouring suite with a soft directional slide, and a tab tap travels the same way, so moving through SonoLune feels like one continuous motion. Crossing between Light and Night keeps its calm cross-fade.
- Every suite icon was redrawn in a softer, more lunar hand, and each tab carries a quiet breathing mark when its suite is holding something, a light field, a loaded atmosphere, an armed Presence Field, an arc due today, or a field held in Mind, so you can wander and still feel what continues.
- Moving never breaks a held space: while Focus or Night leads, a swipe rests the field in place with a gentle word, exactly as a tap does.

## Easier to live with

- A Help & Guides hub gathers a gentle walkthrough for every suite, and each suite now says plainly what it is for.
- Free at heart, and honest about it: the whole core stays free, including the Presence Field over your screen with one chosen app, and a calm, felt-first support dialog opens the deeper refinements only when you reach them. Support keeps SonoLune ad-free, offline, and collecting no data.
- Your sound keeps playing when you swipe SonoLune away from recents, and the app opens faster, its moon waking with a warm, breathing glow.
- Telling us something, a bug, a disturbance, a suggestion, opens one calm in-app note that arrives with the details that help.
- Stress-tested at the largest accessibility fonts and display sizes: every screen holds its full text and layout, card strips fade softly at their edges rather than clipping, and landscape views scroll so nothing sits out of reach.

## A steady first open

- A deep, invisible performance pass teaches the always-breathing touches, the suite bar's glow, Mind's moon and aurora, the warmth at the screen's edges, to redraw only on the frames where their light truly changes and to rest entirely while unseen, so the very same calm runs noticeably smoother and sips less battery on older and lower-end phones, with not one pixel rendered differently.
- The Field Journal, the release archive that greets you on the first open after an update, now always carries you back into SonoLune, whether you use its back arrow, the system gesture, or the Return button, and never leaves you on an empty screen.
- A small steadiness in the support glimpse: the Open Window preview note no longer lingers when its capsule is tapped more than once, it simply refreshes in place.
- A settled Presence Field now holds every suite steady, not just some: a scheduled or tapped Mind Arc answers with the same gentle 'the field is settled; lift it from the Field tab' as Light Arcs and moments already did, rather than quietly tugging its atmosphere against the field.
- And resting the whole environment (the long-press on the Mind moon) now lifts an active Presence Field with it, instead of leaving the field drawn over a sleeping environment with no way back but a manual lift.
