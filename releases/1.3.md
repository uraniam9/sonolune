# SonoLune 1.3

**Major environment update** · 29 Mar 2026

A wide release that expanded sound layering, refined focus sessions, reworked Night, and improved system continuity.

## Highlights

- Neuro Layers and six sound layers
- Ambient Field replaces Focus Bed
- Quick Settings tile for Light Suite
- Last Session card and stronger device compatibility

## Sound Atmosphere

- Neuro Layers added micro textures and stereo width to fill the space around you.
- Drift Duration was added for smoother fades.
- Expanded from three sound layers to six, with additional patches.
- Focus Beam and Sleep Descent were added as new soundscapes.
- Improved smoother playback across all layers.

## Focus Session

- Mind Programmer was brought inside Focus with inline affirmation editing and subliminal settings.
- Ambient Field replaced Focus Bed so you can choose or customize your ambient field.
- Focus toggles were upgraded, including Calm Light, Ambient, Focus Flow, and Greyscale.
- The 30-second closure sequence was enhanced with light, sound, and haptics.
- Focus Session flow and stability improved.

## Light Suite

- UI and UX overhaul for cleaner, more intuitive light controls.
- Added a Quick Settings tile to toggle SonoLune light from Android Quick Settings.

## Night Environment

- Night received a new immersive and intentional UI overhaul.
- Pull to Exit plus Anchor now prevents accidental exits by pulling from the top or holding.
- Fixed Sacred Night Mode crash issues.

## Mind Suite

- Added Stay in the Field page to help you remain anchored.
- Mind Suite session flow was stabilized with performance and UI refinements.

## System and Home Space

- Added Last Session card so you can resume from where you left off.
- Expanded permission helper dialogs and suspended mode helper screens.
- Improved compatibility across more devices.

## Performance and Stability

- Reduced memory usage and optimized animations.
- Glass UI stability improved with no flickers or inconsistencies.
