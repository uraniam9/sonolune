# SonoLune 1.9.0

**Presence Field, one quiet room where sound and screen settle together**

*An Insider build: it went to testers and never reached the public store.*

Presence Field arrives as a new room beside Night: a single co-regulated field where your atmosphere and your screen move as one. As you let the sound deepen, the screen settles with it, a warm, matte quiet drawn over everything, never a wall and never a lock. Arming the field takes a slow two-to-three second descent so you feel it arrive; lifting it returns your screen just as gently. One livable depth is open to everyone, with the continuous depth and the sound-led co-regulation opening first inside your own field before they are ever named. Schedule the field to meet your evening on its own, and lift it instantly with a single touch whenever you want the day back.

## Highlights

- Presence Field: a new top-level room beside Night that draws one warm, matte quiet over the whole screen, a haze that softens, never a filter that blocks.
- Sound ↔ screen co-regulation: let Sound Atmosphere depth rise and the field settles with it on one shared axis, deeper sound, quieter screen.
- A felt arrival: the field descends over two to three seconds when it arms and returns just as softly when you lift it, so you always know it is there.
- One complete, livable depth is open to everyone; the continuous depth slider, co-regulation, and scheduling open first as a thirty-second window in your own field before support is mentioned.
- Settling: the longer you rest, the field may deepen a touch on its own, gently, within a readable floor, and lifted the instant you touch anything.
- Schedule Presence Field through Lunar Arc to meet dusk or wind-down on its own, and lift it any time with one tap, from the room, the notification, or the Quick Settings tile.
- Presence Field always yields to Focus and Night, and steps aside over video, maps, and protected screens.

## The co-regulated field

- Sound Atmosphere depth and the new field depth ride one 0–1 axis, so the screen quiets as the room deepens.
- The quiet is a warm matte haze with a gentle dim and lifted blacks, honest about what it is, and always held above a legibility floor.
- Co-regulation opens for thirty seconds inside your own field before it is ever gated, felt first, asked second.

## Always yours to lift

- A single tap lifts the field, from the room, the ongoing notification, or the Quick Settings tile.
- The field settles back when you return to your phone, and never holds an app hostage.
- Focus and Night always take precedence; the field steps aside for them and for video, maps, and protected screens.
